
# Walking-fly occupancy data

Méndez-Valderrama et al. (2018), Density-functional fluctuation theory of crowds, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-05750-z. Source deposit: https://osf.io/7ubz2/. Original counts, bin maps, source README and reference MATLAB scripts are in `dfft-2018/`. Rows are recorded observations, columns spatial bins.

The companion fits the rectangle data and predicts crowded distributions in the C-shaped and square chambers. Physical bin areas are recorded in `physical-bin-areas.json`; predictions transfer costs at equal physical density. The rectangle filename contains “96flies,” but observed totals are 56–66. C-shaped totals are 2 and 189; square totals are 1 and approximately 125. The staircase files are retained as deposited but are not used in the transfer test. Source observation indices are used when the per-file recording interval is unspecified.
